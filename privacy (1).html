Privacy Policy — Tiktok1

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Data we collect

When you connect a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the basic profile information TikTok provides (such as your username and profile picture) and an access token used to post videos you choose to publish. We do not collect passwords, private messages, or data from other apps.

2. How we use data

Your TikTok profile information is used only to identify the connected account and to post videos through TikTok's Content Posting API. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising.

3. Data storage and security

Access tokens are stored securely on the server that runs the Tiktok1 bot and are used only for posting. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, which immediately stops any further posting.

4. Data retention and deletion

Connected account data is kept only while the account stays connected. If you disconnect your account or ask for deletion, your tokens and profile data are removed. Contact us via TikTok at @ai170490 to request deletion.

5. Children's privacy

Tiktok1 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
